Trophic Diet Compatibility & Cascade Guide

Bipartite interaction network mapping herbivore feeding affinities, trophic specialization, and ecological carrying capacity.

🍽️ Binary Affinities

This bipartite matrix establishes feeding compatibility between herbivores (rows) and flora species (columns):

  • ☑️ Compatible: The herbivore swarm grazes on this host plant when co-located in the same cell, transferring plant biomass into herbivore energy.
  • ☐ Incompatible: The herbivore cannot consume this species. Co-located swarms bypass the plant entirely.
  • Bite Mechanics: Feeding rate, plant digestibility modifiers, and herbivore digestive efficiency modulate exact energy transfers.

🌿 Specialization vs Generalism

Network connectivity directly impacts ecological stability:

  • Generalist Herbivores: Swarms feeding on multiple flora species buffer themselves against single-species collapses, but can drive vulnerable flora species to extinction.
  • Specialist Herbivores: Swarms restricted to a single host exhibit classic tightly-coupled Lotka-Volterra predator-prey oscillations.
  • Trophic Refuges: Incompatible flora species act as competition buffers and physical barriers across the cellular grid.

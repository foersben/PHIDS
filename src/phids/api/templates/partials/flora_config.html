Flora Species & Autotrophic Growth Guide

Comprehensive reference for primary autotrophy, the Decoupled Dual-Proxy Architecture, anemochorous seed dispersal, underground mycorrhizal symbiosis, and cross-view ecosystem interfaces.

⚡ Autotrophy & Dual-Proxy
  • ⚡ Base E: Caloric energy allocated to germinated seedlings ($E_{\text{base}} \ge 0.1$).
  • 🔋 Max E: Photosynthetic carrying capacity ceiling ($E_{\text{max}}$). Surplus energy is funneled toward reproduction or defense.
  • 📈 Growth Rate: Passive autotrophic caloric gain ($g_j$) per tick from photosynthesis.
  • ❤️ Surv. Thr.: Critical starvation floor ($E_{\text{survival}}$); falling below triggers senescence.
  • 🪵 Max Struct ($M_{\text{max}}$): Permanent dry mass ceiling (grams). Decouples current caloric health from trampling vulnerability. 0.0 uses $E_{\text{max}}$ fallback.
  • 🌱 Struct Growth ($g_M$): Lignification rate per 168-tick slow loop stride (default 0.01 = 1%/wk).
🌾 Seed Dispersal & Anemochory
  • 🌱 Repr. Int.: Modulo tick stride ($\Delta t_{\text{repr}}$) for seed reproduction attempts (min 1).
  • 💰 Seed Cost ($E_{\text{seed}}$): Energy reserve deducted from parent upon reproduction. Parents will not drop seeds if remaining energy $\le E_{\text{survival}}$.
  • 🎯 Min / Max Dist: Coordinate bounds ($d \sim \mathcal{U}(d_{\text{min}}, d_{\text{max}})$) for $\mathcal{O}(1)$ polar raycasting dispersal.
  • 🌬️ Drop Height & Fall Velocity: Aerodynamic parameters ($h, v_{\text{term}}$) calculating flight time $t_{\text{fall}} = \frac{h}{v_{\text{term}}}$ and downwind advection $\Delta \vec{x} = \vec{u}_{\text{wind}} \cdot t_{\text{fall}}$.
  • 🚫 Lattice Exclusion: If target coordinate is already occupied by mature canopy, seed cost is expended with zero return.
🤝 Symbiosis & Phloem Kinetics
  • 🍄 Root Tax ($\tau_{\text{link}}$): Continuous caloric upkeep fee paid per active mycorrhizal fungal link per tick. Grazed stubble shedding links prevents fungal parasitism.
  • 🔄 Translocation ($\rho_{\text{trans}}$): Rate of vascular phloem nutrient withdrawal from foliage into below-ground root sinks during herbivore attack.
  • 🎭 Camouflage ($\kappa_{\text{camo}}$): Olfactory masking trait attenuating semiochemical VOC plumes ($C_{\text{eff}} = C \cdot (1 - \kappa_{\text{camo}})$), reducing herbivore chemotactic discovery.
  • 📶 Alarm Relays: Inter-species mycorrhizal root conduits transmit volatile warning signals upstream of physical herbivore contact.
🌐 Implicit Ecosystem Boundaries
  • 🐛 MVT Patch Departure: Governed on Herbivores via Holling Type II intake vs. metabolic upkeep ratio ($\text{Intake} \ge \text{Upkeep}$). Plants provide calories and digestibility; grazers decide departure.
  • 🦬 Herd Trampling: Herbivore plow factor ($k_{\text{incidental}}$) on Herbivores interacts with plant $M_{\text{structural}} / M_{\text{max}}$ to crush fragile seedlings ($P_{\text{crush}} \propto 1 - \frac{M_{\text{structural}}}{M_{\text{max}}}$).
  • 🍽️ Trophic Affinities: Binary feeding links are configured in the Diet Matrix.
  • 🛡️ Spines & Lignin: Mechanical bite damage ($m_{\text{bite}}$) and tissue digestibility ($\mu_{\text{digest}}$) are configured in Morphology & Defense.

Flora Species & Autotrophic Growth Guide

Physiological parameters governing primary biomass synthesis, allometric energy storage, senescence thresholds, and spatial dispersal.

Ecological Parameters Explained:

  • ⚡ Base E (Base Energy): Initial biomass/energy allocated to newly germinated seedlings or placed specimens (min 0.1).
  • 🔋 Max E (Max Energy): Upper carrying-capacity limit per specimen (min 0.1). When energy reaches Max E, photosynthetic intake saturates and excess energy is funneled toward reproduction or defense.
  • 📈 Growth Rate: Passive autotrophic energy gain per simulation tick (min 0.0) from photosynthesis and root nutrient uptake.
  • ❤️ Surv. Thr. (Survival Threshold): Critical energy floor (min 0.0). Falling below this threshold triggers plant senescence (starvation death).
  • 🌱 Repr. Int. (Reproduction Interval): Tick interval dictating mitotic/spatial seed dispersion attempts (min 1).
  • 🎭 Camo (Camouflage): Evolutionary morphological/chemical trait causing localized semiochemical signal attenuation, disrupting the VOC flow-field and reducing herbivore olfactory detection.

Dispersion Mechanics & Draft Lifecycle:

  • 🌾 Seed Dispersion: Plants disperse new clonal or seed entities across neighboring cells within their physiological dispersal radius if sufficient surplus energy exists above their survival threshold.
  • 🤝 Mycorrhizal Energy Relays: Species with active root channels share surplus energy and alarm signals across adjacent cells, buffering vulnerable seedlings against herbivore grazing.
